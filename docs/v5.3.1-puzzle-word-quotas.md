# V5.3.1 puzzle-word quota policy

The reviewed `global-recognition-v4.1.0` registry still contains 103 compositions, but it is reference data, not the runtime action set. The effective gameplay policy is `puzzle-word-quotas-v5.3.1`: only exact sampled spellings are playable, each no more times than its occurrence count. Aliases and group names are not activated from a sampled species or a partial name. Scoring and biological discovery behavior for an accepted composition are unchanged.

For the 105 certified puzzles, `sampledBirdIds` supplies ordered occurrences and is checked against the stored trace and 90-character multiset. For the 15 experimental puzzles, the analogous occurrence list is checked against its trace and characters when the bank loads. The authored tutorial traces supply their own occurrence lists, retaining 小白鹭. Unknown IDs, missing sources, or mismatched words/characters fail explicitly. Runtime state keeps immutable starting quotas and separate remaining counts; retry and puzzle switching reconstruct both from the selected level.

Published `puzzleId`, `boardHash`, certificate hash, generation version, and historical `recognitionRulesVersion` remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Those fields describe the historical certified artifact and cannot honestly be relabeled without changing puzzle identity. `gameplayRulesetVersion` is therefore a non-destructive runtime overlay, also written to new local playtest attempts. Old records remain unmodified and exportable; the statistics panel aggregates only attempts tagged with the current ruleset. The committed V5.1–V5.3 simulation JSON files are historical global-recognition measurements, not current-rule reports.

All 105 certified and 15 experimental traces, plus both tutorials, still replay to victory under the new engine. Matched fixed-budget non-oracle comparisons in `tools/puzzleQuotaComparison.ts` use unchanged IDs, boards, seeds, and strategies. Production capacity-aware wins changed from 2/105 to 46/105; experimental capacity-aware wins changed from 1/15 to 10/15, and experimental bounded-planner wins from 0/15 to 10/15. Other strategies still won 0 in these runs. Mean available compositions at the 30-take certificate checkpoint changed from 0.467 to 0.429. Mean maximum tray occupancy remained 10 for most policies. These are simulated outcomes, not human success estimates, and no arbitrary tile-taking order is guaranteed to win.
