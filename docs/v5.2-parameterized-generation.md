# V5.2 offline parameterized generation

This milestone is experimental. It does not alter the 105 published certificates, the recognition registry, tutorials, scoring, tray, UI, or Pages game. Run `node tools/runParameterizedExperiment.mjs` to regenerate `tools/parameterized-report.json`, or add `--check` to verify byte-for-byte reproducibility. The runner has a 100-second process cap. The fixed matrix uses seeds 31, 37, and 43, every profile × placement combination plus six spatial-order controls, the same four V5.1 non-oracle strategies, and the same 140-action budget. Simulations receive a level with its constructed witness removed. The report uses separate `WON`, `LOST`, `UNKNOWN_BUDGET`, `INVALID_ACTION`, and `ERROR` statuses. None of these labels implies proof of unsolvability.

## Controls and fixed spatial facts

`experimental-difficulty-v1` profiles control the eligible reviewed species pool (familiar/discovery pool sizes), eligible two-/three-/four-character name lengths, distinct-species bounds, per-species occurrence cap, shared-character pair floor/density ceiling, spatial assignment order, and consecutive/paired/multi-name placement. Spatial order is seeded-random, favor-unlocks, or defer-unlocks among currently removable positions. It changes **which characters occupy which positions and the exposure trajectory**, not tile coordinates or blocker density. These are generation constraints, not a weighted difficulty score. The sampler's bounded rejection budget is explicit. Obviously impossible bounds are rejected up front; other unachievable requests return a bounded rejection rather than silently changing the target. Only the reported profile/seed ranges are empirically validated.

Piachan's 90 authored positions are fixed: four layers, 19 initially exposed tiles, 203 blocking edges (0.0507 of all tile pairs), and a 308 × 308 px unscaled board. The validator checks IDs, visual overlap/blocker equivalence, same-layer non-overlap, and mobile width. Tile count, layers, initial exposure, and blocking density cannot be tuned with this geometry. Smaller ~30, 45, 60, or 75-tile layouts are technically possible only as new, independently authored and validated geometries with their own IDs, overlap graph, connectivity/acyclicity tests, mobile bounds, and certification. No smaller geometry was implemented because the current experiment first needed to isolate vocabulary and placement effects; no unvalidated layout was published.

The placement modes use the same sampled birds and topological removal order for a profile/seed. Consecutive placement fills and composes one bird at a time. Paired and multi-name placement interleave characters from up to two or three sampled birds, taking the group's tiles before composing; groups never exceed the ten-slot tray. Every candidate is checked through actual engine transitions to a completed board/tray. Low witness occupancy is a certificate property, **not** an estimate of ordinary player tray pressure.

## Fixed-seed results

The 33 attempted candidates all replay-certified; all 33 board hashes were unique and every content sample succeeded on its first attempt. The 27-cell profile × placement matrix plus six additional spatial-order controls took 10.40 seconds, with a separate 33-candidate generation-and-replay pass taking 0.30 seconds; the report is 84,218 bytes and observed process RSS was about 111 MiB. The first table describes **nine main-matrix cells per profile** (three seeds × three placement modes), not human win rates.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Beginner: 28 familiar, 0 discovery eligible; lengths 2–3; occurrence cap 2 | 0 | 22 / 15.33 / 0 | 20.33 | 14.67 |
| Standard: 24 familiar, 8 discovery eligible; lengths 2–4; cap 3 | 4–6 (mean 5) | 15.33 / 12.67 / 5.33 | 20.33 | 20 |

These settings genuinely shift vocabulary mix and name length. They do **not** monotonically shift every ambiguity measure: mean whole-board competing-composition pairs were 48.67, 51.67, and 51.33 respectively. Name length and distinct-species changes interact; the labels are design targets, not validated human classes.

Initial exposed-tile count remained 19 in every cell, as geometry requires. The initial exposed-character composition upper bound ranged from 2–9 across cells; it is **not** the number playable at once because 19 tiles cannot all fit in the tray. Early legal choices, repeated-character types, whole-board competition, witness checkpoints, trial actions, and trial tray pressure are recorded per cell in the JSON. “Strategically risky legal action” is not assigned a proven count: that would need bounded counterfactual search with UNKNOWN branches kept distinct from proven dead ends.

The six extra spatial controls hold **Standard vocabulary, paired placement, and each seed constant** while changing only assignment order. All three orders yield different hashes for each seed and the same 19 initially exposed positions. At the first witness checkpoint, legal-choice counts were 15/15/15 for seeded-random, 27/24/25 for favor-unlocks, and 8/7/7 for defer-unlocks. This supports controllability of the exposure trajectory, not a conclusion that more choices are easier for people.

Across all 33 candidates, each of the four fixed-seed non-oracle strategies recorded 0 WON, 33 LOST, 0 UNKNOWN_BUDGET, 0 INVALID_ACTION, and 0 ERROR. All certified witnesses used 116–128 actions, so the 140-action cap can accommodate the known winning paths; failure was not caused by a shorter cap. The strong separation in **construction properties** did not produce a reliable separation in simulated success. The strategies are weak proxies for people, and three seeds per cell are too few to establish human difficulty. Keep all three profiles offline. Do not label the public challenge collection Beginner/Standard/Expert yet.

Next evidence needed: more fixed seeds, independently assessed early counterfactual risk with explicit node/time budgets, richer non-oracle strategies, and voluntary human playtests before promoting profiles or designing a smaller geometry. Never promote a candidate merely because its constructed witness has low tray occupancy.
