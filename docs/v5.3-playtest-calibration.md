# V5.3 playtest calibration

The optional **难度实验** selector contains 15 replay-certified boards outside the 105-puzzle production rotation. A/B/C are construction groups, not validated Easy/Medium/Hard tiers. All groups use the standard V5.2 sampling profile, Piachan's immutable 90-tile geometry, the same five seeds (31, 37, 43, 47, 53), the same global 103-composition recognition rules, and a matched sampled character multiset for each seed. Only placement changes: A consecutive, B paired, C multi-name. IDs include generator version, profile, placement, spatial order, seed, and board hash. The stored full assignment and winning trace are independently checked against deterministic regeneration and engine replay. Existing production bank JSON is untouched.

Run `node tools/generatePlaytestBank.mjs --check` and `node tools/runPlaytestComparison.mjs --check` to reproduce identities and outcomes. Both scripts have 90-second child-process limits. Wall-clock generation/replay timings are recorded in the report but excluded from byte-for-byte comparison because they vary by host. The report distinguishes constructed-witness tray occupancy from non-oracle outcomes. The planner evaluates every legal root action, expands at most four roots to depth two with 80 successor nodes per decision, and each trial has a 140-action limit. Its worker has a 90-second timeout. `UNKNOWN_BUDGET` is not a loss. No strategy reads the stored trace.

The comparison in `tools/playtest-comparison.json` has five accepted, replay-certified, unique boards per group, zero rejections, and no overlap with the production bank. Mean maximum tray occupancy *on the constructed witness* is 4.0 / 7.2 / 9.6 for A/B/C. These are structural differences, not human difficulty ratings. All 15 bounded-planner trials in this run ended in genuine engine loss; that does not challenge their winning certificates. Initial exposure is fixed by geometry. Other metrics and per-seed outcomes are in the JSON report. Generation and certification runtimes are local measurements, not guaranteed costs on other machines.

## Local attempt data

Recording is **off by default**. When enabled, the first successful tile take or bird composition starts an attempt. Hidden-tab time is excluded. A win or genuine engine terminal loss ends it. Switching puzzle, tutorial, or group after an action, and retrying, records `ABANDONED`; page unload or a stored in-progress attempt discovered at reload records `INTERRUPTED`. No-action visits create no record. Disabling recording discards the current in-progress attempt. Records stay in `localStorage` under `bird.playtest.v1`, capped at the most recent 500 completed attempts. Quota/security/corruption/unsupported-version errors do not block play. Export requires clicking **导出 JSON** and saves a local file; there is no network submission. **清除记录** removes prior records and the active attempt. The panel reports local sample sizes only.

The data include puzzle/group, attempt number, timestamps, active duration, takes, compositions, peak tray, final score, result, and terminal failure reason. No account, IP, location, free-text, fingerprint, or raw clickstream is collected. Exported files are under the player's control.

## Human calibration later

Ask consenting testers to export JSON voluntarily. Aggregate offline by group and matched seed, retaining attempt count and per-player clustering if players choose to provide an anonymous study code separately; do not upload automatically or infer identity from device data. Compare completion rate, active duration, retries, and tray pressure with confidence intervals and adequate per-group sample sizes. Account for order/learning effects by rotating group order. Keep `ABANDONED` and `INTERRUPTED` distinct from `LOST`. Promote boards or claim player-facing difficulty tiers only after this human evidence supports a stable separation; promotion requires new explicit review and must not mutate historical IDs or certificates.
